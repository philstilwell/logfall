Fallacy profile

Appeal to novelty

Occurs when a proposal is claimed to be superior or better solely based on it being new or modern.

PerspectivalTacticalEmotional

Definition

Occurs when a proposal is claimed to be superior or better solely based on it being new or modern.

Illustrative example

This brilliant strategy that the management has introduced is revolutionary and will change the way we do business!

Why it misleads

A fuller explanation of how the fallacy works and why it can look persuasive.

This fallacy often results from the human tendency for change and new stimuli.

Questions to ask

Prompt 2

Prompt 3
