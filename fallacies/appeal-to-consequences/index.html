Definition

Illustrative example

Case studies

Examples and discussion prompts attached to the original source entry.

It has been frequently argued that there can't possibly be a god since it would mean he has allowed evil and suffering in the world. The mere negative consequences do not deny the possibility of a god. You must also explain why a god would necessarily not permit evil and suffering in the world.
A common argument heard is that, because Hitler based his genocide on evolution, this is an indication that evolution is wrong.
A very common argument for an eternal soul is "If we do not have a soul, then we would be without an objective purpose!" The argument usually ends here with the implication that such a conclusion is unthinkable and unacceptable. An honest thinker dismisses no conclusion due to the emotional discomfort that accompanies it.
