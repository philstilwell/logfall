Demanding a mechanism

Occurs when strong evidence for a phenomenon is rejected solely because the underlying mechanism is still incomplete, disputed, or not yet fully understood.

Rationality danger

Demanding a mechanism threatens rationality by creating confidence moving faster than the evidence: occurs when strong evidence for a phenomenon is rejected solely because the underlying mechanism is still incomplete, disputed, or not yet fully understood. The module should make the user see how confidence, attention, or burden shifts without a matching increase in evidential support.

Primary distortion

confidence moving faster than the evidence.

Category profile

It overstates, understates, cherry-picks, or misallocates the force of evidence.

Dynamics to notice

Watch the trigger, the shortcut, and the payoff. Trigger: the claim enters under the Evidential frame. Shortcut: It overstates, understates, cherry-picks, or misallocates the force of evidence. Payoff: the conclusion becomes easier to accept, reject, or ignore before the relevant comparison has been paid for.

Warning signs

Warning signs: demanding a mechanism often appears when a discussion uses evidential pressure to accelerate belief. Look for a move from a weaker observation to a stronger conclusion.
