Rationality danger

Appeal to tradition threatens rationality by creating a narrow vantage point pretending to be the whole terrain: occurs when a claim or practice is defended mainly because it has a long history, customary status, or familiar place in a community. The module should make the user see how confidence, attention, or burden shifts without a matching increase in evidential support.

Primary distortion

a narrow vantage point pretending to be the whole terrain.

Category profile

It mistakes one standpoint, timeframe, or interpretive frame for a complete evidential view.

Dynamics to notice

Watch the trigger, the shortcut, and the payoff. Trigger: the claim enters under the Perspectival frame with secondary pressure from Evidential, Emotional. Shortcut: It mistakes one standpoint, timeframe, or interpretive frame for a complete evidential view. Payoff: the conclusion becomes easier to accept, reject, or ignore before the relevant comparison has been paid for.

Warning signs

Warning signs: appeal to tradition often appears when a discussion uses perspectival pressure to accelerate belief. Look for a move from a weaker observation to a stronger conclusion.
