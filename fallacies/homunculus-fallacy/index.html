Homunculus fallacy

Occurs when a mind-like inner observer is smuggled in to explain mind-like abilities, thereby postponing rather than solving the explanation.

Category profile

It warps the conceptual map so that distinctions, boundaries, or levels of analysis mislead the inference.

Dynamics to notice

Watch the trigger, the shortcut, and the payoff. Trigger: the claim enters under the Conceptual frame with secondary pressure from Perspectival. Shortcut: It warps the conceptual map so that distinctions, boundaries, or levels of analysis mislead the inference. Payoff: the conclusion becomes easier to accept, reject, or ignore before the relevant comparison has been paid for.

Warning signs

Warning signs: homunculus fallacy often appears when a discussion uses conceptual pressure to accelerate belief. Look for a move from a weaker observation to a stronger conclusion.
