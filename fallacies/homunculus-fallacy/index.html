Homunculus fallacy

Occurs when a mind-like inner observer is smuggled in to explain mind-like abilities, thereby postponing rather than solving the explanation.

ConceptualPerspectival

Definition

Occurs when a mind-like inner observer is smuggled in to explain mind-like abilities, thereby postponing rather than solving the explanation.

Illustrative example

Common warning signs

Warning signs: homunculus fallacy often appears when a discussion uses conceptual pressure to accelerate belief. Look for a move from a weaker observation to a stronger conclusion.
