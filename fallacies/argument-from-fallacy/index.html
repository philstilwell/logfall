Argument from fallacy

Assuming that, if an argument for some conclusion is fallacious, then the conclusion is false.

Evidential

Definition

Assuming that, if an argument for some conclusion is fallacious, then the conclusion is false.

Illustrative example

Tom told me that, because all Cubans are human, and I am a human, therefore I am a Cuban. His argument is invalid, and therefore I am not a Cuban.
