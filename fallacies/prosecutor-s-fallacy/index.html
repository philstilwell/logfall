Prosecutor's fallacy

Assuming that a low probability of false matches means an improbable false match will not be found.

Mathematical

Definition

Assuming that a low probability of false matches means an improbable false match will not be found.

Illustrative example

The probability of winning the lottery without cheating is a million to one, so the winner must have cheated.
