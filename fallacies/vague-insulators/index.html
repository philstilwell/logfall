Vague insulators

Occurs when terms are intentionally chosen for their vagueness in an attempt to make a position unassailable.

Tactical

Definition

Occurs when terms are intentionally chosen for their vagueness in an attempt to make a position unassailable.

Illustrative example

The energy in these pills creates a force that balances the body's essences.

Why it misleads

A fuller explanation of how the fallacy works and why it can look persuasive.

In the example, "energy", "force", "balances" and "essences" are all too vague to submit to scientific scrutiny.

Case studies

Recent or representative examples that help stress-test the pattern in practice.

There are only three sports: bullfighting, motor racing, and mountaineering; all the rest are merely games. ~Ernest Hemmingway The definitions of "sports" and " games" are vague, and make a refutation difficult.
Theists often introduce words such as "faith" and "sin" that have definitions that are contested even within their own sect.
Those promoting alternative medicines often invoke vague terms such as "energy" and "essence" that seem to have no real referent that can be assessed and critiqued.
