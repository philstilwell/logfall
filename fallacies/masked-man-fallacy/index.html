Masked man fallacy

Occurs when the substitution of identical designators in a true statement leads to a potentially false one.

Formal

Definition

Occurs when the substitution of identical designators in a true statement leads to a potentially false one.

Illustrative example

Since I know who my girlfriend is, but don't know who ate all the ice cream, my girlfriend is not the person who ate the ice cream.
