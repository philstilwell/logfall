Fallacy profile

False compromise

Is the assertion that a compromise near the middle of two positions is correct.

Conceptual

Definition

Is the assertion that a compromise near the middle of two positions is correct.

Illustrative example

If the cyclist had been wearing a helmet as he ought to have, my drunk driving would not have killed him. The court should reduce my prison sentence from 10 to 5 years.
