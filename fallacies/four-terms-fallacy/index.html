Four terms fallacy

A categorical syllogism that has four terms.

Formal

Definition

A categorical syllogism that has four terms.

Illustrative example

All children like candy, and all boys are children. So, all girls like candy.

Reference

Legacy number

31

Family

Formal Fallacy

Aliases

None listed

Quick check

If the premises were true, would the conclusion still fail to follow?

Why it misleads

A fuller explanation of how the fallacy works and why it can look persuasive.

While we understand that all girls are also children, this fact needs to be included as an assumption for the argument to be valid.
