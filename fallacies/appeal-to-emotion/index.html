Fallacy profile

Appeal to emotion

Occurs when a position is promoted through the manipulation of emotions, rather than through the presentation of an actual argument.

EpistemicTacticalEmotional

Definition

Occurs when a position is promoted through the manipulation of emotions, rather than through the presentation of an actual argument.

Illustrative example

It would be awesome to know that we were not alone in the universe. I can't help but believe space aliens exist out there somewhere.
