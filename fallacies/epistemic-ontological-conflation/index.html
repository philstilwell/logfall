Fallacy profile

Epistemic/ontological conflation

Occurs when the effects of a belief in a thing are assumed to be evidence for the existence of that thing.

EvidentialPerceptualEpistemic

Definition

Occurs when the effects of a belief in a thing are assumed to be evidence for the existence of that thing.

Illustrative example

Those who believe they are intelligent, out-perform those who think they are not intelligent on IQ tests. Therefore they are more intelligent.

Why it misleads

A fuller explanation of how the fallacy works and why it can look persuasive.

This fallacy is essentially that of ignoring the placebo effect or other mental effects of belief.

Questions to ask

Case studies

Recent or representative examples that help stress-test the pattern in practice.

Many of those who believe in homeopathy are usually heal after taking homeopathy medicine. It's supporters often claim this is proof for the efficacy of the medicine, and ignore possible placebo effects.
Some studies that allegedly show that theists are happier than atheists are extended as evidence for the power of god, rather than the power of belief in god.
