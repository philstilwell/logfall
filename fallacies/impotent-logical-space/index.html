Impotent logical space

Occurs when a view is framed so every possible outcome fits it equally well, leaving no meaningful room for the claim to fail.

Definition

Occurs when a view is framed so every possible outcome fits it equally well, leaving no meaningful room for the claim to fail.

Illustrative example

If the forecast comes true, the psychic was right; if it fails, the warning changed the future, which also proves the psychic was right.

Rationality danger

Impotent logical space threatens rationality by creating confidence moving faster than the evidence: occurs when a view is framed so every possible outcome fits it equally well, leaving no meaningful room for the claim to fail. The module should make the user see how confidence, attention, or burden shifts without a matching increase in evidential support.

Primary distortion

confidence moving faster than the evidence.

Category profile

It overstates, understates, cherry-picks, or misallocates the force of evidence.

Dynamics to notice

Watch the trigger, the shortcut, and the payoff. Trigger: the claim enters under the Evidential frame with secondary pressure from Conceptual. Shortcut: It overstates, understates, cherry-picks, or misallocates the force of evidence. Payoff: the conclusion becomes easier to accept, reject, or ignore before the relevant comparison has been paid for.

Warning signs

Warning signs: impotent logical space often appears when a discussion uses evidential pressure to accelerate belief. Look for a move from a weaker observation to a stronger conclusion.
