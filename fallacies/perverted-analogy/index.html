Perverted analogy

Twisting an opponent's analogy to mean something broader than intended.

ConceptualTactical

Definition

Twisting an opponent's analogy to mean something broader than intended.

Illustrative example

Tom said life is like a river in that those who spend the energy to maneuver their boats to the middle of the river will see more during their lifetime. This is ridiculous since he can not explained where the boats come from.

Case studies

Examples and discussion prompts attached to the original source entry.

If person <i>A</i> claims their "loving" god will eternally torture the humans he created if they disobey him, and person <i>B</i> claims this is analogous to a "loving" human father eternally torturing his children if they disobey, for person <i>A</i> would unjustifiably pervert the analogy if they were to claim that the analogy does hold for irrelevant reasons such as "the human father is not righteous" or "human fathers don't live eternally".
If person <i>A</i> claims religious faith gives people joy that hides and prevents other possible joys in life in the same way that cocaine gives junkies joy at the expense of other possible joys in life, person <i>B</i> would unjustifiably pervert the analogy if they were to claim that the analogy does hold for irrelevant reasons such as "cocaine is not legal" or "people with faith serve God.".
