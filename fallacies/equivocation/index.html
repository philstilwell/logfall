Equivocation

Occurs when a key word or phrase slides between different meanings inside the same argument, creating the illusion of support.

Definition

Occurs when a key word or phrase slides between different meanings inside the same argument, creating the illusion of support.

Why this kind of mistake matters

What to watch for

What to watch for

Watch the trigger, the shortcut, and the payoff. Trigger: the claim enters under the Linguistic frame. Shortcut: It lets ambiguity, framing, or unstable wording do work that evidence or valid inference should do. Payoff: the conclusion becomes easier to accept, reject, or ignore before the relevant comparison has been paid for.

Common warning signs

Warning signs: equivocation often appears when a discussion uses linguistic pressure to accelerate belief. Look for a move from a weaker observation to a stronger conclusion.
