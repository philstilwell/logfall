Naturalistic fallacy

Occurs when something is treated as good, safe, or morally preferable mainly because it is called natural, traditional, or closer to nature.

Why it matters

Why this mistake matters

Naturalistic fallacy threatens rationality by creating bad category architecture: occurs when something is treated as good, safe, or morally preferable mainly because it is called natural, traditional, or closer to nature. Why this kind of mistake matters

It warps the conceptual map so that distinctions, boundaries, or levels of analysis mislead the inference.

What to watch for

What to watch for

Watch the trigger, the shortcut, and the payoff. Trigger: the claim enters under the Conceptual frame with secondary pressure from Evidential. Shortcut: It warps the conceptual map so that distinctions, boundaries, or levels of analysis mislead the inference. Payoff: the conclusion becomes easier to accept, reject, or ignore before the relevant comparison has been paid for.

Common warning signs

Warning signs: naturalistic fallacy often appears when a discussion uses conceptual pressure to accelerate belief. Look for a move from a weaker observation to a stronger conclusion.

How to fix it

How to fix it
