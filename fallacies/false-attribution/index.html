Fallacy profile

False attribution

Occurs when an advocate appeals to an irrelevant, unqualified, unidentified, biased or fabricated source in support of an argument.

EvidentialTactical

Definition

Occurs when an advocate appeals to an irrelevant, unqualified, unidentified, biased or fabricated source in support of an argument.

Illustrative example

A viral post claims a Nobel Prize winner proved a miracle cure works, but the quote is misattributed and the researcher never made the claim.
