Fallacy profile

Wrong causal direction

Occurs when actual cause and effect are mistakenly reversed.

Causal

Definition

Occurs when actual cause and effect are mistakenly reversed.

Illustrative example

I've noticed that people with psychological disorders tend to use recreational drugs. They must be taking drugs to relieve the trauma of their disorders.

Why it misleads

A fuller explanation of how the fallacy works and why it can look persuasive.

Without evidence to indicate causation and the direction of that causation, the coincidence of two phenomena is at best correlation.

Case studies

Recent or representative examples that help stress-test the pattern in practice.

The increase of handguns has often been cited as the cause of increasing crime. Before this claim can be rigorously substantiated, the degree that crime has had on the increase in handguns purchased for increased personal security in the area of increased crime will have to be teased out.
In the middle ages, Europeans noticed that people who got sick had fewer fleas on their bodies, leading them to conclude that fleas prevent illness. The actual mechanism was that an illness was usually accompanied by a fever, which then forced the heat-sensitive fleas to look for another host.
