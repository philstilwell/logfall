Fallacy profile

Package-deal fallacy

Consists of assuming that things often grouped together by tradition or culture must always be grouped that way.

Conceptual

Definition

Consists of assuming that things often grouped together by tradition or culture must always be grouped that way.

Illustrative example

She's likes bowling, so she certainly drinks beer as do most other bowlers.

Quick check

Why it misleads

A fuller explanation of how the fallacy works and why it can look persuasive.

This fallacy is measured in degrees. For example, you might be warranted in saying "John will probably like this book since the last book he enjoyed was also science fiction."
