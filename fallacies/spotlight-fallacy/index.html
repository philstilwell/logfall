Spotlight fallacy

Occurs when a person uncritically assumes that every member within a certain porous category is like those that receive the most coverage in the media.

Perspectival

Definition

Occurs when a person uncritically assumes that every member within a certain porous category is like those that receive the most coverage in the media.

Illustrative example

All the Hispanics I've seen interviewed on television are not the least bit shy, so your neighbor Felipe surely can't be shy!

Case studies

Because of the salient drama surrounding abortion clinic bombings, it became easy to erroneously view all abortion opponents as gun-wielding lunatics.
Some could argue, based on television interviews, that Americans are not at all shy. This wold be a erroneous assumption since those to shy to be interviewed are never seen. This is the same type of error that leads to a selection bias in statistical studies.
While most Japanese who watch their pessimistic media assumed the 1,000 murders in 2010 in Japan was much higher than in what seemed to be a more peaceful past, the number was nearly 3 times lower than the murder rate in the 1950s, and remains one of the lowest per captita murder rates in the world.
