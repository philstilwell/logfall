Top-down faulty generalization

Occurs when an exception to a generalization is wrongly called for.

ConceptualEvidential

Definition

Occurs when an exception to a generalization is wrongly called for.

Illustrative example

Since cutting someone with a knife is a crime, surgeons are criminals.
