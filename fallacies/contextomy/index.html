Contextomy

Occurs when words are selectively excerpted from their original context in a way that changes or distorts what the speaker meant.

TacticalLinguistic

Definition

Occurs when words are selectively excerpted from their original context in a way that changes or distorts what the speaker meant.

Illustrative example

The scientist said the result was 'uncertain,' so he admitted the theory was collapsing.

What to watch for

Shortcut: It moves attention away from the claim's evidential status and toward a pressure tactic, distraction, or rhetorical maneuver. Payoff: the conclusion becomes easier to accept, reject, or ignore before the relevant comparison has been paid for.

Common warning signs

Warning signs: contextomy often appears when a discussion uses tactical pressure to accelerate belief. Look for a move from a weaker observation to a stronger conclusion. Also watch for aliases or nearby labels: quoting out of context.
