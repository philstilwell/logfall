Pathetic fallacy

Occurs when an inanimate object is wrongly treated as if it has the characteristics of animate objects.

ConceptualPerspectival

Definition

Occurs when an inanimate object is wrongly treated as if it has the characteristics of animate objects.

Illustrative example

Look at the flowers lean towards the sun. You're cruel to sit in their sunlight!
