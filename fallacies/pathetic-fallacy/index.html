Pathetic fallacy

Occurs when human feelings, intentions, or judgments are projected onto impersonal things and then treated as if the projection explained reality.

What to watch for

Payoff: the conclusion becomes easier to accept, reject, or ignore before the relevant comparison has been paid for.

Common warning signs

Warning signs: pathetic fallacy often appears when a discussion uses conceptual pressure to accelerate belief. Look for a move from a weaker observation to a stronger conclusion.
